How I work

I've been building with AI since before the hype. I started using ChatGPT when the AI wave was still forming, and it became a core part of how I work. Not as a gimmick -- as a genuine force multiplier across everything I do: coding, editing, copywriting, research, workflow design. Now I use Claude as my primary tool, and it's changed how fast and how well I can deliver.

That means when you send me footage, the repetitive work -- rough cuts, caption generation, color matching, asset organization -- gets handled in a fraction of the time. I spend my hours on the creative decisions that actually matter: pacing, storytelling, hooks, and the feel of the final product.

But video editing is just one side. I'm a generalist who goes deep. I've managed affiliate and creator programs that scaled to hundreds of sales per week. I've built SEO strategies, run social media for brands, written copy that converts, and automated workflows with Python and JavaScript. If your content needs more than just an editor -- strategy, growth, automation -- I can do that too.

Based in Manila (UTC+8). I work US hours, so if you're in North America, I edit while you sleep and you wake up to finished work.
